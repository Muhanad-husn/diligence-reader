Re Group tax structure and US sales/use tax nexus exposure

1. Purpose and summary

This memorandum summarises the Group's tax structure and addresses an identified US sales/use tax
economic-nexus exposure. We estimate the aggregate historical exposure across affected states at
approximately $6m–$9m (including estimated tax, interest and potential penalties), and we set out a remediation
plan to register, file and, where available, mitigate through voluntary disclosure agreements.

2. Group tax structure (overview)

• US parent incorporated in Delaware; principal operating subsidiaries in the US.

• EU operations are conducted through the Irish subsidiary, which acts as the EU data controller and the
contracting entity for EU customers.

• Additional operating entities in the UK, Germany and Singapore support regional sales and engineering.
• Transfer-pricing policy follows a cost-plus / residual-profit-split methodology, documented in the Group's
master and local files.

3. Sales/use tax economic nexus

Following the expansion of economic-nexus rules across US states, certain digital advertising, subscription and
data-service revenues may create sales/use tax collection obligations in states where the Group exceeds the
relevant revenue or transaction thresholds. We have reviewed the Group's revenue footprint and identified a
number of states where registration and collection obligations likely apply and where the Group has not historically
collected.

4. Remediation plan
1 Complete the nexus study and finalise state-by-state exposure quantification (Q1 2026).
2 Register prospectively in states where thresholds are met and commence collection.
3 Pursue voluntary disclosure agreements (VDAs) where available to limit the look-back period and abate
penalties.
4 Implement automated tax-determination software for digital-service revenue.
5 Provide for the estimated exposure in the FY2025 accounts on completion of quantification.
5. Conclusion
The sales/use tax nexus exposure is genuine but bounded and manageable, with a clear remediation path. We
recommend proceeding with registration and the VDA programme and reflecting the estimated exposure in the
accounts as quantification is finalised. This matter is unrelated to the Group's income-tax position, which we
consider well supported.
