Monthly Finance Pack — October 2025. Management accounts and key performance indicators for VistaPort
Media Inc.. Prepared by Victor Osei, Group Controller. Figures are management estimates, unaudited.

1. Executive summary

October trading was broadly in line with plan for most of the month. Advertising impressions softened modestly in
the final week. Trust-and-safety and customer-support costs ran a little above plan late in the month, and the Group
incurred some incremental third-party professional fees. These items are being reviewed; they are not currently
expected to be significant on a full-year basis.

4. Commentary

• Advertising: impressions eased in the last week of the month; cause under review.
• Trust & safety: a minor late-month uptick in cost; engineering and operations time.
• Customer support: contact volumes a touch above plan toward month-end.

• Professional fees: incremental third-party professional fees were incurred in the month (see footnote 1).
