Selected Notes to the Draft Financial Statements

Note 1 — Basis of preparation

Prepared on a basis consistent with the audited FY2024 statements. Comparatives are derived from the audited
FY2024 financial statements. The audit for FY2025 is in progress and these figures remain subject to adjustment.

Note 2 — Adjusted EBITDA

Management presents adjusted EBITDA of $775m, reconciled from reported EBITDA of $612m by add-backs for
items management considers non-recurring or non-operational. The detailed bridge is set out in the adjusted
EBITDA add-back schedule provided in the data room.

Note 3 — Commitments and contingencies

The Group is subject to claims and commitments arising in the ordinary course of business. In addition, a
trust-and-safety contingency of $12m has been recognised within other liabilities and provisions. Management
is monitoring an operational security matter, the outcome of which is not currently estimable. Based on information
available at the date of these draft statements, management considers the amount recognised to be a reasonable
estimate of the obligation that is both probable and reliably measurable; a wider range of possible outcomes exists
but is not, in management's view, currently estimable with sufficient reliability to recognise a further provision.

Note 4 — Subsequent events

Subsequent to year end, the Group incurred costs in connection with an operational account-integrity programme
(referred to internally as the programme), comprising staged account-security activity and
associated customer support and third-party professional fees. These costs are reflected, to the extent incurred in
the period, within operating expenses and the add-back schedule, and are expected to continue at a reduced level
into the early part of FY2026.
