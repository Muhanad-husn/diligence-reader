Analysis of LumenX Ad Exchange marketplace economics for the second half of FY2025, prepared for the
diligence file. Covers take-rate stability, fill rate, and the eCPM yield index across the weeks of 1 September to 29
December 2025.

1. Take-rate is stable

The exchange take-rate (net revenue retained on intermediated media) was stable through H2 FY2025 at
approximately 14.8%, within the 14.5%–15.0% band held for the prior eight quarters. Take-rate is structurally
insulated from short-term demand shifts because it is levied as a percentage of cleared media.
Average take-rate (H2 FY2025) 14.8%
Prior 8-quarter band 14.5%–15.0%

Take-rate trend Flat — no structural change

2. Fill rate and eCPM yield — a Q4 dip

While take-rate held, the marketplace saw a measurable dip in fill rate and the eCPM yield index in the fourth
quarter. The yield index (indexed to 1.000 at the start of the period) softened to an average of 0.953 across the dip
weeks from a baseline of 1.003, recovering partially to 0.973 into late December. Cleared ad impressions eased
from an average of 41.1bn to 38.3bn per week over the same weeks.

2.1 Drivers

Management attributes the dip principally to seasonality (Q4 demand mix and auction dynamics) and to
audience-addressability changes. The addressable logged-in pool refreshed over the quarter — in part reflecting
the staged account-security session refresh — which temporarily reduced the proportion of
impressions carrying a durable logged-in identity signal and therefore softened realised eCPM on addressable
inventory. Non-addressable and contextual yield was comparatively stable. Yield is expected to normalise as the
addressable pool rebuilds.

3. Invalid-traffic (IVT) monitoring
Separately, and unrelated to the yield trend above, LumenX maintains continuous invalid-traffic and ad-fraud (IVT)
monitoring through a third-party MRC-accredited vendor. Measured IVT remained low and within normal bounds in
Q4 (general IVT 0.6%, sophisticated IVT 0.2%), with no material change quarter-on-quarter. IVT is not a contributor
to the Q4 yield softness and is reported here for completeness.
4. Conclusion
• Take-rate is stable and structurally durable.
• Q4 fill-rate and eCPM-yield softness reflects seasonality and audience-addressability changes; addressability
is expected to recover.
•
IVT remains low and is not a driver of the trend.
• No change to marketplace fee structure or supply-path economics.
Yield index and impression figures reconcile to the weekly metric series used across the product and finance materials. Unaudited;
indicative.
