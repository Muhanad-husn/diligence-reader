Subject Strategic partnerships overview

Overview of VistaPort’s strategic distribution, content and identity partnerships, prepared for the diligence file.
These relationships extend reach, supply and addressability beyond owned-and-operated properties.
1. Distribution and device partnerships

• Device OEM placements: pre-installation and default-experience placements for VistaMail and the VistaPort
app across two mid-tier handset OEMs.
• Browser and portal placements: search and content placements with regional portals.
• Connected-TV: content tiles within two CTV platform home experiences.

3. Identity interoperability pilots

VistaPort runs interoperability pilots connecting AtlasID with two industry identity frameworks to improve
cross-publisher match rates as third-party identifiers decline. Pilots are consent-based and privacy-reviewed; early
match-rate results are encouraging and inform the 2026 roadmap (DR-047).
4. Commercial significance
• Partnerships diversify supply and demand and reduce single-property dependency.
•
Identity interoperability is a strategic hedge against signal loss.
• No single partnership in this summary represents a concentration risk on its own.
Search syndication, the one genuine concentration, is summarised separately at DR-034. Pilots remain subject to privacy review
(DR-046, DR-044).
