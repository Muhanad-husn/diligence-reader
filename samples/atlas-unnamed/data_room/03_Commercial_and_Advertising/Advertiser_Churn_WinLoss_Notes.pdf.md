Qualitative win/loss and at-risk notes captured by account teams for the Q4 2025 review. Notes are account-team
vernacular, lightly edited. Used to inform retention motions (DR-033) and the brand-safety feedback log (DR-043).

1. Summary

Net of wins and losses, the book grew in Q4. Most losses were price- or margin-driven or planned exits. A handful
of at-risk notes reference market chatter following the programme account-reset communications; account teams
generally attribute this to competitor FUD and seasonal caution rather than a substantive change in our offer.

4. Themes and actions

• A small rumour trail (‘account resets’, ‘was user data exposed’) is circulating, largely competitor-driven;
counter with the approved Customer Success messaging (DR-042) and Legal-signed-off talking points.
• Most at-risk accounts cite price, budget timing or seasonality as the primary driver.
• Proactively reassure regulated and agency accounts on identity-graph integrity and data-handling continuity.
• Track coded items in the brand-safety feedback log (DR-043) and the retention report (DR-033).
