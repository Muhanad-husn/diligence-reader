These minutes are prepared at the direction of the General Counsel for the purpose of obtaining and providing legal advice and are
intended to be privileged.

1. Purpose

The Committee convened to consider recent operational and security matters and their potential disclosure
implications for the Company, in light of the ongoing strategic process and the Company’s reporting obligations.

2. Background considered

The Committee considered a summary prepared by Security Engineering and the General Counsel’s office
concerning anomalous activity first observed on or around 18 October 2025 affecting a legacy profile backup
associated with the legacy unified account profile store (legacy_uap). The matter had been opened as an internal
investigation and assigned the network-quality ticket; the related forensic workstream is code-named
the workstream.

Date Item noted by the Committee

18 October 2025 Earliest anomalous activity observed against the legacy profile backup (per access-log review).
20 October 2025 Anomalous activity ceased; certain session-validation patterns noted for further review.
21 October 2025 Internal investigation opened by Security Engineering.
23 October 2025 Matter assigned the ticket and managed as a network-quality investigation.
27 October 2025 External forensic adviser engaged.

3. External advisers

The Committee noted that IronLake Forensics had been engaged on an expedited basis to conduct an
independent technical assessment, and that outside privacy and regulatory counsel were available to advise.
Findings from IronLake were awaited and were expected to be material to any assessment.

4. Disclosure assessment

• The Committee concluded that, on the information presently available, the materiality of the matter is not yet
determinable and that any conclusion would be premature pending the IronLake forensic findings.

• Pending those findings, functions were directed to avoid speculative or conclusory terminology (including
characterising the matter as a confirmed breach or exfiltration) in internal and external communications, and to
describe the matter factually by reference to the investigation.

• Any external disclosure, regulatory notification or customer communication should be considered only on the
basis of the forensic findings and on the advice of counsel.

• The Committee deferred the disclosure decision to a subsequent meeting to be reconvened on receipt of
the IronLake assessment.

5. Tension noted

The Committee recorded a discussion between the importance of caution and the need for candour. The Chief
Privacy Officer emphasised that, if the forensic assessment indicated unauthorised access to personal data,
notification obligations (including to the Irish Data Protection Commission as lead supervisory authority) could be
engaged and time-sensitive. The Chair confirmed the Company would act on the advice of counsel once the facts
were established, and that the matter would be kept under active review.
