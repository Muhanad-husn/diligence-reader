# Yahoo Operating Business, Buy-Side Diligence Brief

**Confidential.** These filings are provided to the deal team in connection with the proposed
acquisition of the operating business of **Yahoo! Inc.** ("Yahoo", the "Company") by **Verizon
Communications Inc.** ("Verizon"). Consideration under the original stock purchase agreement:
**US$4,825,800,000 in cash**. Signed 23 July 2016; expected close in 2017. All information is
public record filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission.

---

## Your task

You are the **buy-side diligence lead for Verizon**. Review these filings and produce a
**diligence findings report** for the deal committee. The seller's materials present an
attractive asset (scale, search and communications products, mobile reach, advertising
technology). Your job is to determine whether diligence supports the offer price and terms, or
whether anything in the record should change them.

### Deliverables (in this order)

1. **Executive summary**: your overall recommendation, proceed as-is, proceed with conditions,
   reprice, restructure the consideration (e.g. escrow / special indemnity), or withdraw.
2. **Key findings, ranked by materiality**: for each finding, the issue, the specific document
   IDs (relative file paths) that evidence it, and the financial or legal consequence. Where an
   issue is spread across several filings, or described differently by different filings,
   connect those threads explicitly.
3. **Most material issue, quantification**: your best estimate of the dollar impact, the
   assumptions and calculation behind it, and the **specific deal action** you recommend (e.g. a
   price adjustment of $X, an escrow / special indemnity of $Y, bring-down conditions, revised
   representations).
4. **Lesser, lower-priority issues**: items that are real but smaller, with why they are less
   material.
5. **Confidence and open items**: what you would still need to confirm, and how it could move
   your conclusion.

### Save your report

When you have completed your analysis, **save your findings report as a Markdown (`.md`) file**
(for example, `Diligence_Findings_Report.md` in this folder) containing all of the deliverables
above, in the order listed. This saved Markdown report is your final deliverable.

### Ground rules

- Base every conclusion on the filings here and **cite them by path** (e.g.
  `documents/10-K-2016/11-item-1a-risk-factors.md`).
- Be specific and quantified. Take a position where the filings support one, rather than
  deferring everything to "further review".
- Do not assume facts not evidenced here; where the record is incomplete, say so and state what
  you would request.
