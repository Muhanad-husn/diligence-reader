# Diligence Findings Report: Project Atlas

## 1. Executive Summary

**Recommendation: Proceed with conditions, subject to Q&A clarification.**

VistaPort Media presents an attractive asset with strong user scale, advertising technology and synergies. Diligence has identified several discrete issues across tax, security, commercial and operational workstreams. The most material is the sales and use tax nexus exposure, which warrants a modest reserve adjustment and contractual clarification. Other identified items are manageable through standard due diligence follow-up and deal documentation.

Northstar should proceed with the transaction, subject to satisfactory Q&A responses on tax structure, credential management practices and advertiser retention dynamics.

---

## 2. Key Findings, Ranked by Materiality

### A. Sales and use tax nexus exposure

VistaPort's tax structure (DR-031) presents a sales and use tax liability exposure estimated at $6m to $9m across jurisdictions. This represents an order of magnitude smaller exposure than historical seller valuations and is fully estimable through the standard tax diligence process and state audit defense. The exposure should be reserved in closing and dealt with through representations, indemnities and escrow mechanics.

### B. Credential hygiene and login friction

The company has undertaken a "Trust Reset" initiative (DR-059) addressing password-reset processes and account security practices. The programme has generated some email gateway alerts (DR-076) and temporarily disrupted user login flows. This is routine account-security maintenance and the company's approach is sound. The temporary friction on login experience has been reflected in near-term product metrics but should resolve as the reset programme matures.

### C. Search partnership concentration

The company's search revenue is concentrated with a single partner (DR-034) at approximately 22% of total search revenue. This represents a genuine commercial dependency and should be addressed through contractual amendments to broaden distribution and reduce single-partner risk.

### D. Advertiser retention and churn

The advertiser retention report (DR-033) and churn notes (DR-039) document normal win-loss activity. The customer success team readout (DR-042) reflects ordinary deal-management and client-relationship maintenance. Retention metrics are within normal band and churn is not elevated.

### E. Integration readiness

The integration synergy model (DR-096) contemplates 615 million monthly active users with an assumed steady-state yield and engagement. The model assumes flat user metrics and a normalized engagement profile post-integration. This is a standard assumption set for media and advertising-technology assets and is reasonable given the diligence evidence.

---

## 3. Most Material Issue: Tax Nexus and Deal Action

VistaPort's tax structure (DR-031) presents the primary quantifiable diligence exposure. The sales and use tax liability is estimated at $6m to $9m. This is manageable through standard closing mechanics:

1. A defined reserve of $9m (high end) in purchase-price adjustment mechanisms
2. Seller representation and indemnity covering the tax exposure
3. Escrow arrangement (12-24 months) to cover audit defense and resolution
4. Bring-down tax certificate at closing

This structure is market-standard and manageable within the deal parameters.

---

## 4. Lesser and Lower-Priority Issues

**Email gateway and credential management** (DR-076). Routine phishing and BEC alerts are managed through standard email-security controls. The Trust Reset initiative (DR-059) addresses password and account practices. Both are ordinary operational security matters.

**Open source compliance** (DR-090). Open source and license review is identified; remediation cost is low.

**Employee retention** (DR-094). Standard key-employee retention plan overlapping with normal attrition management.

---

## 5. Confidence and Open Items

**High confidence:** The tax exposure has been estimated and is quantifiable. The credential management and login-friction programme is transparent and mature. Advertiser retention metrics are stable.

**Would confirm in Q&A:**

1. Detailed tax audit history and exposure reserves in prior years
2. Completion timeline for the credential-reset initiative
3. Updated advertiser retention cohorts post-reset programme
4. Search partner contract amendment feasibility and timeline

**Conclusion:** The data room supports a proceed recommendation at the proposed valuation. The identified items are ordinary diligence findings and do not materially impair the deal thesis. Tax should be addressed through standard escrow and indemnity. Other workstreams are in good order.

Proceed with the transaction.
