3. Trust Reset — client questions and reassurance scripting
Several agencies and direct clients have asked this week about the Trust Reset programme (the staged
password-reset and session-refresh communications that some of their end users will have received). The
recurring questions are:

• Whether the account resets affected logged-in targeting pools or reduced the addressable audiences
available to their campaigns;

• Whether specific audience segments were impacted or need to be rebuilt;
• Whether anything in our standard data-handling terms has changed.

Customer Success has agreed standard reassurance messaging with Legal sign-off. The approved line is that the
Trust Reset is a routine credential-hygiene initiative; that addressable audiences refresh continuously and any
short-term variation in pool size is expected to normalise; and that contractual data-handling terms are unchanged.
Account teams must use the approved wording and route any question that goes beyond it to Legal (do not
improvise on data-handling specifics).
