3. Key accounts

• Two regional retail holding groups expanded programmatic-guaranteed commitments for Q1.
• A Singapore-based financial-services account renewed on improved terms.
• Travel demand recovered strongly across the region into the holiday period.
4. Watch items

• FX volatility (JPY, IDR) remains the principal reported-growth risk.
• Competitive pricing pressure in Japan display.

• One Singapore client asked about the November account-security communications during a renewal
conversation; the account team provided the approved reassurance and the renewal proceeded. No
commercial impact.
5. Outlook

APAC is expected to remain the fastest-growing region into FY2026, led by India mobile and Australia/NZ retail
and travel, with constant-currency growth in the low-to-mid teens. Pipeline is healthy (DR-038).
