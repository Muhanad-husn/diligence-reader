3. Information security reporting line
The Chief Information Security Officer, Owen Bell, leads the information-security organisation. For governance
and independence reasons the CISO reports into the General Counsel (Priya Raman) rather than into Technology
or the CFO, with a standing dotted-line escalation path to the Risk Committee of the Board. This arrangement is
intended to keep security assurance close to the Company’s legal and compliance function.
