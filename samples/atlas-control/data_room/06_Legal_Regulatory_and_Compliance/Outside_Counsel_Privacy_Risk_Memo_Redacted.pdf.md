1. Scope and caveats

You have asked for our preliminary view of the potential legal and financial exposure associated with the
account-integrity matter and the anomalous activity observed against a historical legacy profile dataset. This
memorandum is necessarily preliminary: the forensic review is ongoing, the scope of any data acquisition is not
settled, and the figures below are indicative ranges, not estimates of probable loss. They are provided to assist
the client in assessing reserves and strategy and are subject to revision as facts develop.

2. Affected-population assumptions

The exposure analysis is highly sensitive to the number of affected individuals. On the current understanding, the
dataset in question comprises approximately 912.8m historical profile records in total, of which approximately
286m relate to accounts active within the last twenty-four months. The regulatory and notification analysis below
scales principally with the 286m active-account figure, while certain litigation theories could reference the larger
912.8m population. Our quantification methodology and the sensitivity of each band to that determination are set
out at ■■■■■■■ [Annex B, redacted].
