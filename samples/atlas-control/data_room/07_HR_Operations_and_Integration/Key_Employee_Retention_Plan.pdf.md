Strictly confidential. This Key Employee Retention Plan (the “Plan”) sets out the proposed arrangements to retain
critical talent at VistaPort Media Inc. (the “Company”) through completion of the proposed transaction (Project
Atlas) and a defined post-completion stabilisation period. The Plan is a routine pre-signing retention programme of
the kind customary on a transaction of this nature.

1. Objectives

• Retain executives and senior individual contributors whose continuity is important to business performance
and a smooth integration.

• Mitigate ordinary deal-related flight risk during the period of uncertainty between announcement and
completion.

• Align retained leaders to clearly defined completion and integration milestones.

2. Plan structure

Awards comprise a blend of restricted stock awards (RSAs), cash retention bonuses and milestone-linked top-ups.
Awards vest in tranches subject to continued employment and, for the integration tranche, achievement of defined
Day-1 and Day-100 readiness milestones (see the Day-1 Readiness Plan, DR-097).
