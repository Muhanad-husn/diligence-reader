Meeting Meeting of the Board of Directors of VistaPort Meida Inc.
Date & time 11 December 2025, 14:00 (held by video-conference)
Chair Margaret Ellison
Secretary Diane Foster, Corporate Secretary

Quorum A quorum being present, the Chair declared the meeting open.

Apologies: Paul Brenner sent apologies, which were noted.

Minutes of the previous meeting. The minutes of the Q3 2025 meeting, having been circulated, were approved
as a correct record and signed by the Chair. There were no matters arising not otherwise covered by the agenda.

1. Trading update

The Chief Financial Officer presented the Q4 and preliminary full-year FY2025 position. Group revenue for FY2025
is expected to be approximately $5,150m, with adjusted EBITDA of approximately $775m. The Board noted some
softness in advertising impressions and email engagement late in the year, which management attributed to
seasonality and short-term operational factors, with recovery expected into Q1 2026.

2. Strategic process update

The VP Corporate Development reported continued constructive engagement with a strategic counterparty and
progress on data-room readiness. The Board supported the planned process timetable into the first half of 2026.

3. Technology and information security update

The General Counsel introduced an update from the Chief Information Security Officer. Management reported an
account integrity workstream underway during the quarter, together with a Trust Reset programme of staged
credential-hygiene measures (password resets and session refresh) intended to maintain account hygiene across
the estate. These were described as operational matters being managed by the security and technology functions.
The Board noted the operational impact on near-term engagement metrics referred to under item 1.
The General Counsel further noted increased legal review of a network-quality matter identified earlier in the
quarter, in respect of which external advisers had been engaged to support the Company’s assessment. The
internal workstream is referred to under the codename AURORA. Management’s current view, which the Board
noted, is that the materiality of the matter is not yet determined and that it would be kept under review as further
information becomes available. The Board asked to be updated at the next meeting and as appropriate in the
interim.

4. Risk

The Risk Committee Chair noted the items above had been discussed at committee and that the Committee was
satisfied that the matters were being managed with appropriate external support. The Committee would continue to

The Chief Human Resources Officer noted the development of key-employee retention arrangements ahead of the
