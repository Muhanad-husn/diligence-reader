Quorum A quorum being present, the Chair declared the meeting open.

Apologies: Paul Brenner sent apologies, which were noted.

Minutes of the previous meeting. The minutes of the Q3 2024 meeting, having been circulated, were approved
as a correct record and signed by the Chair. There were no matters arising not otherwise covered by the agenda.

1. Trading and financial update

The Chief Financial Officer presented the Q4 and full-year FY2024 trading position. Group revenue for FY2024 is
expected to be approximately $4,990m, ahead of the prior year, with advertising and subscriptions the principal
drivers. Reported EBITDA remained robust and cost discipline measures introduced earlier in the year were
tracking to plan. The Board noted the resilience of the LumenX advertising business and continued growth in
subscriptions.

2. Capital allocation

The Board reviewed the capital-allocation framework. Management recommended continued reinvestment in the
identity graph (AtlasID) and advertising technology, the maintenance of a conservative balance sheet, and a
modest continuation of the existing buyback programme. The Board approved the framework as presented.

3. Strategy

The Chief Executive Officer summarised the medium-term strategy: deepen logged-in engagement, expand
first-party addressable advertising and modernise the legacy technology estate. The Board supported the direction
and requested a fuller strategy review in 2025.

4. Risk

The Risk Committee Chair provided a routine update. The principal risks remained competition for advertising
