This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement and Clean-Team Protocol (this “Agreement”) is entered into between
VistaPort Media, Inc.. (“Discloser”) and the approved Bidder (“Recipient”) in connection with Project Atlas.
1. Confidential Information

“Confidential Information” means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of the Discloser, in any form,
including the existence and terms of the proposed transaction, business and financial information, customer and
user data, and all analyses derived therefrom.
2. Obligations of the Recipient
• Use the Confidential Information solely to evaluate the proposed transaction.
• Limit disclosure to representatives with a need to know who are bound by equivalent duties.
• Maintain at least the same degree of care as for the Recipient’s own confidential information, and in no event
less than a reasonable degree of care.

• Not contact any employee, customer, supplier, regulator or adviser of the Discloser regarding the transaction
without prior written consent.

3. Clean-Team Protocol (personal and competitively sensitive data)
Certain materials contain user-level personal data and competitively sensitive information. Access to such
materials is restricted to nominated “Clean-Team Members”.
• Clean-Team Members must be identified in writing and countersign this protocol.
• User-level personal data may be reviewed only within the data room environment and may not be extracted,
copied or combined with the Recipient’s own datasets.
• Outputs derived from clean-team materials may be shared with the broader deal team only in aggregated,
anonymised or redacted form.

• On request or on termination, clean-team work papers must be destroyed or returned, subject to legal-hold
and regulatory retention requirements.
4. Personal data and data protection

Each party shall comply with applicable data-protection laws. The parties acknowledge that the Discloser remains
the controller of any personal data made available and that the Recipient processes such data only as a limited
reviewer for the permitted purpose. Nothing in this Agreement requires the Discloser to disclose personal data in
breach of law or of its obligations to data subjects or third parties.
5. Term, residuals and remedies

This Agreement remains in force for two (2) years from the date of last disclosure. The Recipient may use general
knowledge, skills and experience retained in unaided memory (the “residuals”), provided this clause does not

and that injunctive relief may be sought for breach.
