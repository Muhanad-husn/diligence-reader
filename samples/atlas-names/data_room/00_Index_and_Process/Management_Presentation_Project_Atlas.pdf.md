1. Executive summary

• Scale: ~1.1bn registered accounts and 615m monthly active users across the portfolio.
• Mobile reach: 408m mobile monthly active users and 82m daily active email users.
• Financial strength: FY2025 revenue of $5.15bn and adjusted EBITDA of $775m.
•
Identity & ad-tech: the AtlasID identity graph and the LumenX Ad Exchange power addressable,
privacy-aware advertising at scale.

• Value creation: estimated synergy NPV of $410m when combined with a telecoms customer base.
2. Franchise overview

vistaport operates a portfolio of leading consumer destinations and a full programmatic advertising stack. Daily
engagement spans communication (VistaMail), information (News, Sports, Finance) and search, monetised
through owned-and-operated inventory and the LumenX exchange. The franchise benefits from durable brand
recognition and a logged-in audience that supports first-party, addressable advertising.

3. AtlasID — the identity advantage

AtlasID resolves authenticated users across properties to deliver durable, consented audience signals as
third-party identifiers decline. Combined with a telecoms partner’s first-party customer base, AtlasID can extend
addressable reach and improve match rates across channels — a core element of the value thesis.
4. LumenX — advertising technology

• End-to-end programmatic stack spanning supply, demand and an owned exchange.
• Stable take-rate with scope to expand higher-margin first-party data products.

5. Financial highlights

FY2025 revenue of $5.15bn (up from $4.99bn in FY2024) with adjusted EBITDA of $775m. Revenue is diversified
across product lines:

Adjusted EBITDA reflects management add-backs for non-recurring items (see DR-025). Cash generation

6. Growth strategy

• Deepen logged-in engagement and grow first-party addressable audiences.
• Expand LumenX demand and higher-margin data products.
• Modernise the technology estate, including identity and authentication platforms.
• Grow mobile and international monetisation.

7. Combination thesis & synergies

Combining vistaport’s 615m-user franchise and ad-tech with NORTHSTAR MOBILE HOLDINGS PLC’s telecoms customer
base is expected to unlock approximately $410m of synergy NPV through enhanced addressability, cross-sell and
cost efficiencies (see the integration synergy model, DR-096).
Synergy lever Description

Addressability uplift Higher match rates from combined first-party data
Cross-sell Advertising and subscription cross-sell to telecoms base
Cost efficiencies Infrastructure, procurement and G&A rationalisation
