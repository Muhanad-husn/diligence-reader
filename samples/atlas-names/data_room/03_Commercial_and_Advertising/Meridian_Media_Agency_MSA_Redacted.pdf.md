4. Data Protection, Security & User Trust

4.1 Safeguarding warranties. Vista-Port warrants that it maintains a documented information-security programme
aligned to recognised industry standards and that it implements appropriate technical and organisational measures
to safeguard end-user personal data and the integrity of the audience data used to deliver the Services, including
access controls, encryption of data at rest and in transit, logging and monitoring, and a documented
incident-response capability.

4.2 Notification of Security Incidents and user-trust events. Vista-Port shall notify the Customer without undue
delay, and in any event in accordance with Section 4.5, on becoming aware of (a) any Security Incident affecting
the Customer’s campaigns, end-user personal data processed in connection with the Services, or Vista-Port
systems used to deliver the Services; or (b) any other material event affecting user trust or the integrity of
audience data, including any unauthorised access to, or compromise of, the identity or authentication systems

underpinning addressable audiences. “Security Incident” means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorised
access to, acquisition of, disclosure of, loss of, or interference with end-user personal data or audience data.
4.3 Service credits. Where a Security Incident or material user-trust event materially affects the Services or the
addressable audiences delivered to the Customer, the Customer shall be entitled to service credits calculated in

4.4 Termination for cause. The Customer may terminate this Agreement, or any affected Insertion Order, with
immediate effect on written notice if a Security Incident or material event affecting user trust or the integrity of
audience data occurs and is not remediated to the Customer’s reasonable satisfaction within thirty (30) days of
notification, or where Vista-Port fails to provide notification as required by Section 4.2.
4.5 Notification timing and content. Notification under Section 4.2 shall be made without undue delay and in any
event within seventy-two (72) hours of Vista-Port becoming aware of the relevant event, and shall include a
description of the event, the data and audiences affected (to the extent known), and the remediation steps taken or
planned.

4.6 Audit rights. Not more than once per calendar year (or more frequently following a Security Incident), the
Customer may, on reasonable notice, audit Vista-Port’s compliance with this Section 4, including review of relevant
policies, certifications and incident records, subject to confidentiality and to Vista-Port’s reasonable security
requirements.

Sections 4.2, 4.4 and 4.5 operate together: a qualifying event triggers a 72-hour notification duty, service-credit exposure, and a
customer termination right if the matter is not remediated. The credential-hygiene programme referred to internally as the Trust Reset
is an operational initiative; whether any reportable event has occurred under Section 4.2 is a matter for Legal.
