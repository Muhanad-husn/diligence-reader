Summary of the standard data-processing terms (the “DPA Terms”) that VISTAPORT agrees with EU/EEA advertising
customers, alongside the master services agreement. Prepared by the Privacy Office for the diligence file. This is a
summary; the executed DPA prevails.

1. Roles of the parties

The DPA Terms allocate roles under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR). For most advertising processing each
party acts as an independent controller of the personal data it determines the purposes and means of
processing. Where VISTAPORT processes personal data on a customer’s documented instructions (for example,
customer-provided audience lists), VISTAPORT acts as a processor and the customer as controller, and the Article
28 obligations below apply.

2. Article 28 processor obligations

Where VISTAPORT acts as processor, the DPA Terms incorporate the obligations required by Article 28 GDPR,
including:

• Process personal data only on the controller’s documented instructions.
• Ensure persons authorised to process are bound by confidentiality.
•
Implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures (Article 32).
• Engage sub-processors only with authorisation and on equivalent terms; maintain a sub-processor list.
• Assist the controller with data-subject requests and with security, breach-notification and DPIA obligations.
• Delete or return personal data at the end of the services, subject to legal retention.
• Make available information necessary to demonstrate compliance and submit to audits.

3. Personal-data breach notification

The DPA Terms require VISTAPORT to notify the customer of a personal-data breach affecting the customer’s
personal data without undue delay and in any event within 48-72 hours of becoming aware of the breach. The
notification shall describe, to the extent known, the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of
data subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to address it.
The notification duty is triggered by VISTAPORT becoming aware of a qualifying event, and is intended to enable the
customer to meet its own regulatory obligations (including any Article 33 notification to a supervisory authority).

4. Audit rights

The customer may audit VISTAPORT’s compliance with the DPA Terms, including by reviewing third-party
certifications and audit reports, and (on reasonable notice and subject to confidentiality) by conducting or
commissioning an audit, with enhanced rights following a personal-data breach affecting the customer’s data.
5. International transfers

Transfers of EU/EEA personal data outside the EEA are made under an adequacy decision or the EU Standard
Contractual Clauses with a transfer risk assessment, as set out in the DPA Terms.
