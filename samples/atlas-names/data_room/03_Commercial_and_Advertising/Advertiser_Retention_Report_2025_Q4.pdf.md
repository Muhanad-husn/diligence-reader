3. Churn analysis

Gross churn of 7.9% (revenue) was within the historical 7–9% band. The largest single loss (Solace-adjacent
streaming reseller) was a planned exit on margin grounds. No vertical showed structural deterioration. Win-back
motions are tracked separately in the win/loss notes (DR-039).
3.1 Client questions on user-trust and targeting pools

A small, time-boxed cluster of client questions arose in November 2025 following the Trust Reset
credential-hygiene communications (the staged password-reset and session-refresh programme described in
product materials). A number of agencies asked whether the November login-reset noise had affected logged-in
targeting pools or audience segments, and a few raised general privacy questions. Two mid-size agency accounts
briefly paused incremental bookings pending account-team reassurance.

Account teams, with Customer Success and Legal sign-off on the standard messaging (see DR-042), confirmed
that addressable audiences refresh continuously and that no change to contractual data-handling terms had
occurred. Activity from the affected accounts had substantially recovered by mid-December. We assess this as
