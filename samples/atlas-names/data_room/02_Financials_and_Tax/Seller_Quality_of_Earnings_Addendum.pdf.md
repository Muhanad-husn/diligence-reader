Seller Quality of Earnings — Addendum. Prepared by Cantno Street Capital as financial adviser to VISTAPORT
Media Inc., in support of the FY2025 adjusted EBITDA presentation. This addendum supplements the draft
FY2025 financial statements and the adjusted EBITDA add-back schedule provided in the data room.

1. Purpose

This addendum sets out the basis on which management's adjusted EBITDA of $775m is derived from reported
EBITDA of $612m, and explains why the adviser considers the adjustments appropriate and the underlying
earnings to be of high quality. It is intended to assist bidders in normalising FY2025 performance.

2. Summary view

In our view FY2025 underlying earnings are robust and the adjustments are well supported. The principal
normalising items are non-recurring and clearly identifiable, and the late-year softness in advertising yield reflects a
temporary, programme-related effect rather than any structural change in the franchise.

3. Treatment of the account-integrity / Trust Reset costs

FY2025 includes approximately $34m of costs associated with the account-integrity programme (the Trust Reset
initiative) — comprising staged credential-hygiene activity, incremental customer support and third-party
professional fees. We are satisfied that these costs are non-recurring in nature: the programme is a discrete,
time-bounded operational exercise, and the associated support and professional-fee elevation is not expected to
persist into the normalised run-rate. We have accordingly treated the full $34m as a non-recurring add-back in the
adjusted EBITDA bridge.

4. Normalisation of advertising yield

Advertising yield softened in the fourth quarter in connection with the same programme-related friction. For
normalisation purposes we have restated Q4 delivered yield to pre-October levels, on the basis that the softness is
temporary and yield is expected to revert. The effect of this normalisation is set out below.
