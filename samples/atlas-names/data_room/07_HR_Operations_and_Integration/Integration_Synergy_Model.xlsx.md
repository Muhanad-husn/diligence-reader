Combination thesis: vistaport’s 615m-user franchise and ad-tech combined with the acquirer telecoms customer base. Base-case synergy NPV of $410m (see the management presentation DR-005 and the strategic-alternatives deck DR-015).

The base case assumes the autumn-2025 “Trust Reset” credential-hygiene programme produced no lasting friction and that advertising yield fully normalises to the pre-October index of 1.00. A buyer should test these two assumptions against realised post-October metrics; see the sensitivity tabs. The realised-low scenario reflects the worst observed weeks in the Q4 2025 user-metrics series.

Synergy NPV is geared to the INCREMENTAL logged-in addressable audience and its yield, not to headline MAU. Scenario NPV = Base NPV x [1 - VolGear x (1 - volume factor)] x [1 - YldGear x (1 - yield factor)]. At base (factors = 1) the impacts are 1 and NPV = the base $410m. Cells B10..B16 are live formulas.

Because the synergy thesis rests on addressability of the logged-in pool, a sustained engagement/yield shortfall compounds: the realised-low inputs reduce scenario NPV well below the base case, i.e. several hundred $m of value at risk if the Trust Reset period leaves lasting friction.

Realised-low inputs are the worst observed weeks in the Q4 2025 user-metrics series (DR-048): total MAU and mobile MAU troughs and the minimum advertising-yield index.
