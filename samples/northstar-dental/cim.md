# Confidential Information Memorandum Summary

## Northstar Dental Group

Northstar Dental Group is a regional dental services organization headquartered in Columbus, Ohio.

## Investment highlights

- Operates 5 markets across the Midwest.
- Diversified service mix across general dentistry, hygiene, orthodontics, and implants.
- Management reports strong 2025 performance and claims **18.0% year-over-year revenue growth**.
- Revenue is supported by recurring insurance payor relationships and increasing self-pay implant demand.
