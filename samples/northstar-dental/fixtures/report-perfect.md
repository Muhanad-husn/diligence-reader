# Diligence Findings Report: Northstar Dental Group

## 1. Executive Summary

**Recommendation: Reprice or condition on growth restatement.**

Northstar Dental Group is an attractive regional platform with established payor relationships and diversified clinical services. However, diligence has identified a material inconsistency between the management-reported revenue growth and the workbook figures. The CIM claims 18.0% year-over-year revenue growth (cim.md), but the revenue workbook shows 2024 revenue of $22.2M and 2025 revenue of $24.8M (revenue_summary.xlsx), equating to 11.7% growth. This gap of 630 basis points will impact valuation multiples and deal economics and must be resolved before closing. The deal should either restate the growth rate to 11.7% with repricing adjustment, or be conditioned on updated management guidance. Secondary issues involving vendor concentration and payor dependency are material but addressable through standard due diligence mechanics and representations.

---

## 2. Key Findings, Ranked by Materiality

### A. Revenue growth inconsistency

The CIM states 18.0% year-over-year revenue growth (cim.md), citing strong 2025 performance. However, the revenue summary workbook (revenue_summary.xlsx) provides detailed category-by-category revenue data showing 2024 revenue of $22.2M and 2025 revenue of $24.8M, representing growth of 11.7%. The Q1 board update confirms the $24.8M 2025 figure (board_materials/q1-board-update.md). This 630 basis point gap between claimed and actual growth rate requires explanation. The discrepancy is material to deal valuation and synergy assumptions.

### B. Practice management software dependency and renewal risk

CloudDent Practice Systems is the practice management software. The agreement summary describes it as the "Single system of record for scheduling, claims, and treatment plans." (vendor_agreements/clouddent-practice-systems.md). The risk register adds that the contract has limited termination flexibility (risks/risk_register.md). The CloudDent contract renews on 2025-09-30 (vendor_agreements/clouddent-practice-systems.md), creating near-term renewal risk. The risk register identifies this as high severity (risks/risk_register.md). Failure to renew, or renewal on worse terms, could disrupt scheduling, claims and treatment planning across the clinics.

### C. Payor concentration

The two largest insurance payor relationships, Buckeye Family Health Plan and Midwest Dental Benefits Network, together represent $10.0M of 2025 revenue (risks/risk_register.md). Buckeye alone contributes $5.8M annually (customer_contracts/buckeye-family-health-plan.md) with renewal in March 2026. Midwest contributes $4.2M (customer_contracts/midwest-dental-benefits-network.md) with renewal at year-end 2025. Together these payors represent approximately 40 percent of total revenue and create concentration risk if either relationship is disrupted or repriced.

### D. Midwest Dental payment cycle delays

The Midwest Dental Benefits Network contract notes slower-than-average claims payment cycle (customer_contracts/midwest-dental-benefits-network.md). This may create working capital pressure and should be modeled in the integration plan.

---

## 3. Most Material Issue: Revenue Growth Restatement and Deal Action

### The Discrepancy

Management's CIM narrative states 18.0% year-over-year revenue growth. The detailed revenue workbook shows:
- 2024 revenue: $22.2M (general dentistry $12.4M, hygiene $3.6M, orthodontics $3.2M, implants $3.0M)
- 2025 revenue: $24.8M (general dentistry $13.7M, hygiene $4.1M, orthodontics $3.4M, implants $3.6M)
- Actual growth rate: 11.7%

This is confirmed by the Q1 board update which cites $24.8M total 2025 revenue. The gap between 18.0% and 11.7% represents 630 basis points of growth rate variance.

### Valuation Impact

A business valued on an EV/Revenue multiple is highly sensitive to growth rate. If the deal assumes 18.0% growth as a feature of synergy or base-case underwriting, restating to 11.7% will reduce value accordingly. The precise impact depends on the buyer's valuation methodology, but the restatement will require either a price reduction or a repricing of the initial offer.

### Recommended Deal Action

The purchase agreement should require management to restate the revenue growth from 18.0% to 11.7%, effective upon closing. The purchase price should be adjusted downward by an amount reflecting the valuation impact of 630 basis points of growth-rate correction, or the deal should be conditioned on this restatement with price protection clauses. Alternatively, the seller could provide updated EBITDA guidance or margin assumptions to demonstrate that the lower growth rate does not impair deal economics, but management has not provided such detail.

---

## 4. Lesser and Lower-Priority Issues

**CloudDent renewal terms**: While critical to operations, the CloudDent renewal is a known event in September 2025 and can be managed through negotiation. The diligence should confirm that termination and data portability provisions allow transition to an alternative platform if pricing or service terms are unreasonable.

**Payor concentration and renewal timing**: Buckeye's March 2026 renewal and Midwest's December 2025 renewal create staggered risk. Both contracts should be reviewed for renewal flexibility and pricing escalation caps. The Midwest payment cycle issue should be resolved through better internal working capital management or through contractual amendment to accelerate payment.

**SmileSupply vendor dependency**: SmileSupply Distribution handles supply chain for consumables, but the renewal is not until June 2026 and the relationship is marked as medium risk. Supply diversification should be pursued but is not urgent.

---

## 5. Confidence and Open Items

**High confidence:** The revenue discrepancy is documented in contemporaneous financial records (2024 and 2025 workbook data in revenue_summary.xlsx and the Q1 board update), and represents a straightforward factual inconsistency. The 11.7% growth rate is calculated directly from workbook line items.

**Moderate confidence:** Management's intent in stating 18.0% is unclear. This may reflect projection, a calculation error, or rounding. A management interview should clarify whether the 18.0% is a forward-looking assumption or a misstatement of historical performance.

**Would confirm:**
1. Management explanation for the 18.0% year-over-year revenue growth claim in the CIM.
2. Updated 2025 and 2026 financial guidance reflecting 11.7% historical growth trajectory.
3. CloudDent renewal negotiation status and terms by September 2025.
4. Buckeye and Midwest payor renewal terms and pricing escalation provisions.
5. Detailed loss history and claims experience for both large payor relationships.

The primary recommendation stands: restate revenue growth to 11.7% and adjust deal economics accordingly before closing.
