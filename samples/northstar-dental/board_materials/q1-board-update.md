# Q1 Board Update — Northstar Dental Group

## Executive summary

Northstar Dental Group continues to expand across regional markets while facing operational pressure from vendor dependency, payor concentration, and provider recruiting.

## 2025 revenue by category

- General dentistry: $13.7M
- Hygiene: $4.1M
- Orthodontics: $3.4M
- Implants and cosmetic: $3.6M

Total 2025 revenue: $24.8M

## Board discussion items

1. Reconcile revenue growth language in management materials against the revenue workbook.
2. Review CloudDent renewal terms before 2025-09-30.
3. Evaluate revenue-cycle delays tied to Midwest Dental Benefits Network.
4. Prioritize provider recruiting in Indianapolis and Louisville.
