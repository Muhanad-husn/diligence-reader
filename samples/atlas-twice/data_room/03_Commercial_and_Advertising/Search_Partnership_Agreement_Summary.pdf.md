Summary of the principal third-party search-syndication partnership (the “Partnership”) under which VistaPort
syndicates search queries and monetises results through the partner’s advertising marketplace. Prepared for the
diligence file. Commercial terms are summarised; the executed agreement is held by Legal.
1. Parties and structure
VistaPort entity VistaPort Media Inc. (Delaware)

Counterparty Tier-1 global search-marketplace partner (name redacted in summary)
Structure Revenue-share on syndicated search advertising
Effective 1 April 2022

Renewal Initial term to 31 March 2027, auto-renew 2yr

2. Economics and concentration

The Partnership is the single largest contributor to search monetisation. In FY2025 it accounted for approximately
22% of search revenue (and roughly 6% of total advertising-related revenue). This is a genuine concentration
consideration: search revenue of $1,480m in FY2025 means a material dependency on a single counterparty for
syndicated demand.

Metric FY2023 FY2024 FY2025
Search revenue ($m) 1,402 1,455 1,480
Partnership share of search 20% 21% 22%
Partnership contribution ($m) 280 306 326

3. Key terms (summary)

• Revenue-share tiers based on syndicated query volume and realised RPM.
• Service-level commitments on query latency and availability.
• Brand-safety and content-policy alignment obligations on syndicated results.
• Termination for convenience on 180 days’ notice after the initial term.
• No exclusivity preventing VistaPort from operating its owned search experience.
4. Diligence considerations

• Concentration: ~22% of search revenue depends on one partner; renewal falls in 2027.
• Pricing risk: revenue-share tiers reset at renewal; partner has scale leverage.
• Mitigation: owned-and-operated search and LumenX demand reduce single-point dependency.
• No change-of-control consent is required for the proposed transaction under the summary terms.
