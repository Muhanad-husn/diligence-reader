1. Purpose

This memorandum records the operation of the Company's disclosure controls and procedures and the
materiality-review and escalation process by which potential disclosure items are identified, assessed and
resolved. It also records one item that remains open and is explicitly carried forward pending further information.

2. Process summary

1 Functions escalate potential disclosure items to the Office of the General Counsel as they arise.
2 The General Counsel screens items for potential materiality and refers qualifying items to the Disclosure
Committee.

3 The Committee assesses materiality on the information available, taking advice from outside counsel where
appropriate, and records a decision or, where information is incomplete, an explicit deferral with a review
date.
4 Decisions and deferrals are minuted and revisited as facts develop.

4. Open escalation item — account-integrity matter
An item concerning a cyber and account-integrity matter (the account-integrity workstream referred to internally as
AURORA, and the associated credential-hygiene programme) has been escalated to the Committee. The
Committee considered whether, and if so when, the matter must be disclosed — to counterparties, regulators and
in the Company's financial reporting — and concluded that it could not resolve that question on the present
information.

Specifically, the Committee noted that: (a) the forensic review is ongoing and the scope of any data access has not
been determined; (b) the materiality of the matter is not yet determinable; and (c) outside counsel's assessment is
not complete. The Committee therefore left the disclosure question open, pending completion of the forensic
review and receipt of counsel's advice, rather than concluding either that disclosure is required or that it is not.

Carry-forward and review
• The disclosure question on the account-integrity matter is carried forward as an open item and is to be
revisited promptly on receipt of the final forensic findings and outside-counsel advice.
•
In the interim, functions are to continue to handle the matter in accordance with counsel's guidance and to
avoid speculative characterisation.
• The Committee will reconvene on this item ahead of any transaction milestone that would require a
representation on the subject.
This memorandum records an unresolved question; it is not a conclusion that disclosure is or is not required. Privileged and
confidential.
