1. Summary

Between December 2025 and early January 2026 the security organisation experienced an elevated level of senior
voluntary attrition relative to its historical run-rate. The Chief Information Security Officer, Owen Bell, resigned with
effect from December 2025, and three senior security engineers gave notice over the following weeks. The
departures are concentrated in the authentication and key-management teams. The affected roles are designated
key for retention purposes (see DR-094) and are being actively backfilled.

operational matter.

The “AURORA” review and the related “Trust Reset” programme were referenced by a minority of leavers as the period during which

4. Assessment

HR’s assessment is that the attrition reflects a combination of an intense operational period, a competitive external
market and individual career decisions, compounded by the uncertainty customarily associated with a sale
process. While the concentration of departures in the authentication and key-management teams is notable, we
have not identified a systemic people-management issue. The internal-alignment theme is being noted to the
General Counsel for awareness; it is a matter of professional judgement on the handling of the October event
rather than a conduct concern.
5. Actions in hand

1 Interim leadership cover in place; external CISO search initiated.
2 Backfill requisitions opened for all four roles; two interim specialists engaged.
3 Targeted retention awards extended to remaining senior security engineers under the Key Employee
Retention Plan (DR-094).

4 Knowledge-transfer plan for authentication and key-management runbooks before effective dates.
5 Pulse check of the remaining security organisation scheduled for Q1 FY2026.
