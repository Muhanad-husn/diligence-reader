Analysis of LumenX Ad Exchange marketplace economics for the second half of FY2023, prepared for the
diligence file. Covers take-rate-SE stability, fill rate, and the eCPM yield index across the weeks of 1 September to 29
December 2023.

1. Take-rate-SE is stable

The exchange take-rate-SE (net revenue retained on intermediated media) was stable through H2 FY2023 at
approximately 21.6%, within the 21.2%–21.9% band held for the prior eight quarters. Take-rate-SE is structurally
insulated from short-term-SE demand shifts because it is levied as a percentage of cleared media.
Average take-rate-SE (H2 FY2023) 21.6%
Prior 12-quarter band 21.2%–21.9%

Take-rate-SE trend Flat — no structural change

3. Fill rate and eCPM yield — a Q4 dip

While take-rate-SE held, the marketplace saw a measurable dip in fill rate and the eCPM yield index in the fourth
quarter. The yield index (indexed to 1.460 at the start of the period) softened to an average of 1.391 across the dip
weeks from a baseline of 1.464, recovering partially to 1.421 into late December. Cleared ad impressions eased
from an average of 60.0bn to 55.9bn per week over the same weeks.

3.1 Drivers

Management attributes the dip principally to seasonality (Q4 demand mix and auction dynamics) and to
audience-addressability-SE changes. The addressable logged-in-SE pool refreshed over the quarter — in part reflecting
the staged credential-hygiene-SE (Confidence Rebuild) session refresh — which temporarily reduced the proportion of
impressions carrying a durable logged-in-SE identity signal and therefore softened realised eCPM on addressable
inventory. Non-addressable-SE and contextual yield was comparatively stable. Yield is expected to normalise as the
addressable pool rebuilds.

4. Invalid-traffic-SE (IVT) monitoring
Separately, and unrelated to the yield trend above, LumenX maintains continuous invalid-traffic-SE and ad-fraud-SE (IVT)
monitoring through a third-party-SE MRC-accredited-SE vendor. Measured IVT remained low and within normal bounds in
Q4 (general IVT 0.9%, sophisticated IVT 0.3%), with no material change quarter-on-quarter-SE. IVT is not a contributor
to the Q4 yield softness and is reported here for completeness.
6. Conclusion
• Take-rate-SE is stable and structurally durable.
• Q4 fill-rate-SE and eCPM-yield-SE softness reflects seasonality and audience-addressability-SE changes; addressability
is expected to recover.
•
IVT remains low and is not a driver of the trend.
• No change to marketplace fee structure or supply-path-SE economics.
Yield index and impression figures reconcile to the weekly metric series used across the product and finance materials. Unaudited;
indicative.
