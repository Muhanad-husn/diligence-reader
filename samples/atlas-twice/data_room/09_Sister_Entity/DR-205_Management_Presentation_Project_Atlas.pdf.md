3. Franchise overview

Beaconvale operates a portfolio of leading consumer destinations and a full programmatic advertising stack. Daily
engagement spans communication (VistaMail), information (News, Sports, Finance) and search, monetised
through owned-and-operated-SE inventory and the LumenX exchange. The franchise benefits from durable brand
recognition and a logged-in-SE audience that supports first-party-SE, addressable advertising.

4. AtlasID — the identity advantage

AtlasID resolves authenticated users across properties to deliver durable, consented audience signals as
third-party-SE identifiers decline. Combined with a telecoms partner’s first-party-SE customer base, AtlasID can extend
addressable reach and improve match rates across channels — a core element of the value thesis.
6. LumenX — advertising technology

• End-to-end-SE programmatic stack spanning supply, demand and an owned exchange.
• Stable take-rate-SE with scope to expand higher-margin-SE first-party-SE data products.

9. Growth strategy

• Deepen logged-in-SE engagement and grow first-party-SE addressable audiences.
• Expand LumenX demand and higher-margin-SE data products.
• Modernise the technology estate, including identity and authentication platforms.
• Grow mobile and international monetisation.

10. Combination thesis & synergies

Combining Beaconvale’s 898m-user franchise and ad-tech-SE with Eastridge Mobile Holdings plc’s telecoms customer
base is expected to unlock approximately $599m of synergy NPV through enhanced addressability, cross-sell-SE and
cost efficiencies (see the integration synergy model, DR-096-SE).
Synergy lever Description

Addressability uplift Higher match rates from combined first-party-SE data
Cross-sell-SE Advertising and subscription cross-sell-SE to telecoms base
Cost efficiencies Infrastructure, procurement and G&A rationalisation

12. Transaction summary
