1. Executive summary

December showed a partial recovery from the November Confidence Rebuild friction. Customer-support-SE contact volumes
normalised toward the end of the month and advertising impressions improved. However, email weekly active
users and mobile monthly active users both remained modestly below plan at year end: engagement recovery has
been slower than modelled.

6. Commentary

• Support: contact volumes normalising; temporary contact-centre-SE capacity wound down.
• Advertising: impressions recovering toward the prior run-rate-SE but not yet fully back.

• Engagement: email WAU and mobile MAU still below plan; recovery slower than modelled.
• Costs: trust-and-safety-SE and professional fees lower than November but above baseline.
