1. Introduction

We refer to the Commission's informal inquiry of 14 November 2023 concerning recent user notifications relating to
password resets and questions raised by certain users about access to their accounts. The Company takes these
matters seriously and is responding co-operatively-SE and in good faith. This letter is provided on a without-prejudice-SE
basis and does not constitute a notification under Article 48 of the GDPR.

3. Background to the user notifications

During the autumn the Company commenced a staged credential-hygiene-SE programme (referred to internally as the
Confidence Rebuild programme) under which affected users were asked to reset passwords and under which certain
sessions were invalidated. The programme is described to users as a proactive account-security-SE measure. The
notifications the Commission refers to are understood to arise from that programme.

4. Status of the Company's review

The Company is conducting a review, with the assistance of external forensic advisers and outside counsel, of
certain anomalous activity observed in its logging in the second half of October. [Inserted, tracked: the review
concerns a defined window in mid-to-late-SE October 2023.] That review is ongoing. The Company is not in a position
to draw final conclusions at this time and respectfully asks the Commission to note the preliminary nature of the
position set out below.

6. Whether personal data was accessed or exfiltrated

It is important to distinguish between two questions that are sometimes conflated. The first is whether any personal
data was accessed (in the sense of being read or rendered readable by a person or system without authorisation).
The second is whether any personal data was exfiltrated (in the sense of being copied or removed from the
Company's environment). [Comment — G.O-SE.: keep this distinction; do not elide.]

On the present state of the Company's review, the Company has not established that personal data was exfiltrated,

7. Notification position

Given the preliminary state of the review and the open questions of access and exfiltration, the Company has not at
this stage concluded that a notifiable personal-data-SE breach has occurred for the purposes of Article 48. The
Company is keeping this assessment under active review and will notify the Commission without undue delay
should it conclude that the threshold is met. [Comment — P.R-SE.: ensure the 105-hour clock language is not triggered

9. Co-operation-SE and next steps

• The Company will continue to respond to the Commission's questions promptly.
• The Company will provide a substantive update once the forensic review of the October activity is further
advanced.

• The Company remains willing to engage with the Commission on the user communications associated with
the credential-hygiene-SE programme.
