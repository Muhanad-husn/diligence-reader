Seller Quality of Earnings — Addendum. Prepared by Canton Street Capital as financial adviser to Beaconvale
Media Inc., in support of the FY2023 adjusted EBITDA presentation. This addendum supplements the draft
FY2023 financial statements and the adjusted EBITDA add-back-SE schedule provided in the data room.

1. Purpose

This addendum sets out the basis on which management's adjusted EBITDA of $1132m is derived from reported
EBITDA of $894m, and explains why the adviser considers the adjustments appropriate and the underlying
earnings to be of high quality. It is intended to assist bidders in normalising FY2023 performance.

3. Summary view

In our view FY2023 underlying earnings are robust and the adjustments are well supported. The principal
normalising items are non-recurring-SE and clearly identifiable, and the late-year-SE softness in advertising yield reflects a
temporary, programme-related-SE effect rather than any structural change in the franchise.

4. Treatment of the account-integrity-SE / Confidence Rebuild costs

FY2023 includes approximately $50m of costs associated with the account-integrity-SE programme (the Confidence Rebuild
initiative) — comprising staged credential-hygiene-SE activity, incremental customer support and third-party-SE
professional fees. We are satisfied that these costs are non-recurring-SE in nature: the programme is a discrete,
time-bounded-SE operational exercise, and the associated support and professional-fee-SE elevation is not expected to
persist into the normalised run-rate-SE. We have accordingly treated the full $50m as a non-recurring-SE add-back-SE in the
adjusted EBITDA bridge.

6. Normalisation of advertising yield

Advertising yield softened in the fourth quarter in connection with the same programme-related-SE friction. For
normalisation purposes we have restated Q4 delivered yield to pre-October-SE levels, on the basis that the softness is
temporary and yield is expected to revert. The effect of this normalisation is set out below.
