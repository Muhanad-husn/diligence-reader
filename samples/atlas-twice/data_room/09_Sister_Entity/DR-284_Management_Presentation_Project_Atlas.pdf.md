1. Executive summary

• Scale: ~0.7bn registered accounts and 394m monthly active users across the portfolio.
• Mobile reach: 261m mobile monthly active users and 52m daily active email users.
• Financial strength: FY2020 revenue of $3.30bn and adjusted EBITDA of $496m.
•
Identity & ad-tech-SG: the AtlasID identity graph and the LumenX Ad Exchange power addressable,
privacy-aware-SG advertising at scale.

• Value creation: estimated synergy NPV of $262m when combined with a telecoms customer base.
1. Franchise overview

Calderwood operates a portfolio of leading consumer destinations and a full programmatic advertising stack. Daily
engagement spans communication (VistaMail), information (News, Sports, Finance) and search, monetised
through owned-and-operated-SG inventory and the LumenX exchange. The franchise benefits from durable brand
recognition and a logged-in-SG audience that supports first-party-SG, addressable advertising.

2. AtlasID — the identity advantage

AtlasID resolves authenticated users across properties to deliver durable, consented audience signals as
third-party-SG identifiers decline. Combined with a telecoms partner’s first-party-SG customer base, AtlasID can extend
addressable reach and improve match rates across channels — a core element of the value thesis.
3. LumenX — advertising technology

• End-to-end-SG programmatic stack spanning supply, demand and an owned exchange.
• Stable take-rate-SG with scope to expand higher-margin-SG first-party-SG data products.

3. Financial highlights

FY2020 revenue of $3.30bn (up from $3.19bn in FY2019) with adjusted EBITDA of $496m. Revenue is diversified
across product lines:

Product FY2020 revenue % of total
Search $947m 18.4%

Adjusted EBITDA reflects management add-backs-SG for non-recurring-SG items (see DR-025-SG). Cash generation

4. Growth strategy

• Deepen logged-in-SG engagement and grow first-party-SG addressable audiences.
• Expand LumenX demand and higher-margin-SG data products.
• Modernise the technology estate, including identity and authentication platforms.
• Grow mobile and international monetisation.

4. Combination thesis & synergies

Combining Calderwood’s 394m-user franchise and ad-tech-SG with Southgate Mobile Holdings plc’s telecoms customer
base is expected to unlock approximately $262m of synergy NPV through enhanced addressability, cross-sell-SG and
cost efficiencies (see the integration synergy model, DR-096-SG).
Synergy lever Description

Addressability uplift Higher match rates from combined first-party-SG data
Cross-sell-SG Advertising and subscription cross-sell-SG to telecoms base
Cost efficiencies Infrastructure, procurement and G&A rationalisation

5. Transaction summary

Management welcomes discussions on a transaction valuing the operating business at approximately $3.09bn
enterprise value, reflecting the scale, mobile reach and ad-tech-SG assets described herein.
