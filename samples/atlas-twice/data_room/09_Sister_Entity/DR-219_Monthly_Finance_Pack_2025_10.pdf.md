1. Executive summary

October trading was broadly in line with plan for most of the month. Advertising impressions softened modestly in
the final week. Trust-and-safety-SE and customer-support-SE costs ran a little above plan late in the month, and the Group
incurred some incremental third-party-SE professional fees. These items are being reviewed; they are not currently
expected to be significant on a full-year-SE basis.

6. Commentary

• Advertising: impressions eased in the last week of the month; cause under review.
• Trust & safety: a minor late-month-SE uptick in cost; engineering and operations time.
• Customer support: contact volumes a touch above plan toward month-end-SE.

• Professional fees: incremental third-party-SE professional fees were incurred in the month (see footnote 1).
