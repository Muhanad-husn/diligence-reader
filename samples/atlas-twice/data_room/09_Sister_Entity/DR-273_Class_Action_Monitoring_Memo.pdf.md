1. Purpose and scope

This memorandum surveys recent privacy and data-breach-SE class-action-SE activity in the consumer-internet-SE sector
and summarises publicly reported settlement analogues. Its purpose is to assist the Company in understanding the
range of outcomes seen in comparable matters. It does not assert that the Company has been, or will be, the
subject of any such claim; no consumer class action has been filed against the Company (see the litigation
schedule, DR-078-SE).

3. Sector trends

• Plaintiff firms have become more active in filing consumer-privacy-SE and data-breach-SE class actions promptly
following public reports of large-scale-SE credential resets or account-access-SE events.
• Theories of harm increasingly rely on statutory damages and risk-of-harm-SE frameworks rather than proof of
out-of-pocket-SE loss.

• Multidistrict consolidation is common where multiple firms file in parallel.
• Settlements frequently bundle cash funds with credit-monitoring-SE and injunctive security-enhancement-SE
commitments.
