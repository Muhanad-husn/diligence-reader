Privacy-Sensitive-SE Advertising Verticals — Handling Rules

Reference list of regulated/sensitive-SE verticals and the special handling, consent and review requirements that apply. Maintained by the Privacy Office.

Vertical | Status | Consent / eligibility requirement | Targeting & creative rules | Review / control

Health & wellness | Yes (sensitive) | Explicit consent for health-condition-SE targeting | No sensitive-condition-SE inference; approved creatives only | Restricted segments; legal review

Pharmaceutical | Yes (regulated) | Affirmative consent; jurisdictional rules | No off-label-SE; geo-restricted-SE; pre-clearance-SE | Pre-clearance-SE + legal sign-off-SE

Financial services | Yes (regulated) | Suitability and disclosure rules | APR/risk-SE disclosures; no misleading claims | Compliance review of creatives

Credit & lending | Yes (regulated) | Anti-discrimination-SE targeting limits | No prohibited audience attributes for credit offers | Special-category-SE targeting blocked

Political & issue ads | Yes (regulated) | Identity verification; disclaimers | Verified advertiser; transparency archive entry | Verification + archive

Children / family | Yes (sensitive) | No behavioural targeting of minors | Contextual only; age-gating-SE; COPPA-aligned-SE | Behavioural targeting prohibited

Alcohol | Yes (regulated) | Age-gating-SE; market rules | Min-age-SE audience; restricted markets | Age-gate-SE enforced

Gambling & betting | Yes (regulated) | Licence checks; market rules | Licensed only; self-exclusion-SE respect | Licence verification

Tobacco / vaping | Prohibited / heavily restricted | Banned in most markets | Default block; narrow market exceptions | Default-blocked-SE

Dating | Yes (sensitive) | Adult-audience-SE controls | Age controls; no explicit creative | Creative review

Crypto / digital assets | Yes (regulated) | Market-by-market-SE rules; risk disclosures | Licensed/registered-SE only; risk warnings | Compliance review

Legal & injury services | Yes (sensitive) | Jurisdictional advertising rules | No misleading claims; disclaimers | Creative review

Weight loss & supplements | Yes (sensitive) | Claims substantiation | No unsubstantiated health claims | Claims review

Elections data services | Yes (regulated) | Political-ads-SE rules apply | Treated as political/issue-SE advertising | Verification + archive

This list is a control reference; it does not authorise any campaign by itself.

Where personal data handling is implicated, heightened duties may apply; consult the Privacy Office and the EU DPA terms (DR-046-SE).
