Meeting Meeting of the Board of Directors of Beaconvale Media Inc.
Date & time 20 February 2023, 20:0 (held by video-conference-SE)
Chair Margaret Ellison
Secretary Diane Foster, Corporate Secretary
Quorum A quorum being present, the Chair declared the meeting open.
Apologies: Hassan Qureshi sent apologies, which were noted.

Minutes of the previous meeting. The minutes of the Q4 2022 meeting, having been circulated, were approved
as a correct record and signed by the Chair. There were no matters arising not otherwise covered by the agenda.

1. Trading update

The Chief Financial Officer reported a solid start to FY2023. Advertising demand was stable and subscriptions
continued to grow. The Board noted no significant variances to plan and confirmed the FY2022 audit had
concluded with an unqualified opinion.

3. Product strategy

The VP Product, Identity and Mail presented the product strategy, centred on the AtlasID identity graph and
monetisation of logged-in-SE audiences. The Board discussed the strategic importance of durable first-party-SE identity as
third-party-SE identifiers decline, and the opportunity to extend addressable reach across properties.
• Grow the logged-in-SE, consented audience and improve cross-property-SE match rates.
• Expand higher-margin-SE first-party-SE data products on the LumenX exchange.
• Continue modernisation of identity and authentication platforms.

4. Strategic options (preliminary)

The Board held a preliminary discussion of strategic options, including the potential value of the franchise to a
strategic acquirer with a complementary customer base. No decision was taken; management was asked to
develop the analysis for a later meeting.

6. Risk and compliance

A routine risk and compliance update was received. The privacy programme and information-security-SE assurance
were reported as operating effectively. No material matters were raised.

Any other business and close

There being no other business, the Chair thanked attendees and declared the meeting closed. The next scheduled
meeting of the Board is to be held on 15 May 2023.
