1. Purpose

This memorandum records the operation of the Company's disclosure controls and procedures and the
materiality-review-SE and escalation process by which potential disclosure items are identified, assessed and
resolved. It also records one item that remains open and is explicitly carried forward pending further information.

3. Process summary

1 Functions escalate potential disclosure items to the Office of the General Counsel as they arise.
3 The General Counsel screens items for potential materiality and refers qualifying items to the Disclosure
Committee.

4 The Committee assesses materiality on the information available, taking advice from outside counsel where
appropriate, and records a decision or, where information is incomplete, an explicit deferral with a review
date.
6 Decisions and deferrals are minuted and revisited as facts develop.
