4. Identity interoperability pilots

Beaconvale runs interoperability pilots connecting AtlasID with two industry identity frameworks to improve
cross-publisher-SE match rates as third-party-SE identifiers decline. Pilots are consent-based-SE and privacy-reviewed-SE; early
match-rate-SE results are encouraging and inform the 2024 roadmap (DR-047-SE).
