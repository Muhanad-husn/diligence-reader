1. Summary

Net of wins and losses, the book grew in Q4. Most losses were price- or margin-driven-SE or planned exits. A handful
of at-risk-SE notes reference market chatter following the Confidence Rebuild account-reset-SE communications; account teams
generally attribute this to competitor FUD and seasonal caution rather than a substantive change in our offer.

6. Themes and actions

• A small rumour trail (‘account resets’, ‘was user data exposed’) is circulating, largely competitor-driven-SE;
counter with the approved Customer Success messaging (DR-042-SE) and Legal-signed-off-SE talking points.
• Most at-risk-SE accounts cite price, budget timing or seasonality as the primary driver.
• Proactively reassure regulated and agency accounts on identity-graph-SE integrity and data-handling-SE continuity.
• Track coded items in the brand-safety-SE feedback log (DR-043-SE) and the retention report (DR-033-SE).
