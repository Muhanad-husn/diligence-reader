1. Executive summary

November was affected by short-term-SE operational friction associated with the Confidence Rebuild programme (staged
password resets and session refresh). This temporarily reduced advertising impressions and email engagement
and drove a spike in customer-support-SE contacts relating to account lockouts and password resets. Third-party-SE
professional fees remained elevated. Management regards these effects as temporary and operational, and
expects engagement to recover.
