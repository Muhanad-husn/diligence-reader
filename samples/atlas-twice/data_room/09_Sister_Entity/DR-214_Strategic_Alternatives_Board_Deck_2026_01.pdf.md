Strategic Alternatives — Board Discussion

Prepared by Corporate Development for the Board of Beaconvale Media Inc.. This deck summarises the indicative
valuation and strategic rationale for a potential combination with Eastridge Mobile Holdings plc (“Eastridge”),
together with alternatives. Strictly confidential; for Board discussion only.
Indicative and illustrative. Figures are unaudited and subject to confirmatory diligence and definitive documentation.

1. Situation

• Eastridge has indicated a non-binding-SE interest at an indicative enterprise value of approximately $7.05bn.
• The strategic rationale rests on combining Beaconvale’s logged-in-SE audience and advertising technology with a
complementary telecoms customer base.

• A competitive process is being prepared to test value, with the data room and management presentation in
advanced preparation.

4. Value drivers

• Scale & engagement: approximately 898m monthly active users and 596m mobile MAU underpin the
audience proposition.
•
Identity & addressability: the AtlasID identity graph supports durable first-party-SE addressable advertising as
third-party-SE identifiers decline.

• Ad-tech-SE monetisation: the LumenX exchange provides a take-rate-SE on large advertising flows with scope for
higher-margin-SE first-party-SE data products.

• Synergies: estimated synergy NPV of approximately $599m from addressability uplift, cross-sell-SE and cost
efficiencies when combined with the Eastridge base (see the integration synergy model).

9. Alternatives considered

• Status quo / continue as independent: retains optionality but foregoes near-term-SE value crystallisation and
synergy capture.

• Partial disposal / partnership: narrower value; greater execution complexity.
• Sale of the operating business (recommended to test): the Eastridge indication provides an attractive
starting point to run a competitive process.
10. Key risks and considerations
• Advertising market and macro conditions affecting near-term-SE yield.
• Customer-concentration-SE and renewal timing in the top advertiser cohort.
• Execution and regulatory approvals on any transaction.

• One open workstream (account integrity) is being managed by management and counsel; it is kept under
review and is not expected, on current information, to be quantified at this stage.
• Confirmatory diligence may identify items requiring further analysis.
12. Recommendation

Corporate Development recommends that the Board authorise management to progress a competitive process to
test the indicative $7.05bn valuation, while continuing to prepare the data room and maintaining optionality on
alternatives. A further update will be brought to the Board following receipt of indicative offers.
