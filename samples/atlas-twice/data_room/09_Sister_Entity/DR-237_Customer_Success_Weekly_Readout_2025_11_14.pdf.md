4. Confidence Rebuild — client questions and reassurance scripting
Several agencies and direct clients have asked this week about the Confidence Rebuild programme (the staged
password-reset-SE and session-refresh-SE communications that some of their end users will have received). The
recurring questions are:

• Whether the account resets affected logged-in-SE targeting pools or reduced the addressable audiences
available to their campaigns;

• Whether specific audience segments were impacted or need to be rebuilt;
• Whether anything in our standard data-handling-SE terms has changed.

Customer Success has agreed standard reassurance messaging with Legal sign-off-SE. The approved line is that the
Confidence Rebuild is a routine credential-hygiene-SE initiative; that addressable audiences refresh continuously and any
short-term-SE variation in pool size is expected to normalise; and that contractual data-handling-SE terms are unchanged.
Account teams must use the approved wording and route any question that goes beyond it to Legal (do not
improvise on data-handling-SE specifics).
