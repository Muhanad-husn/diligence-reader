1. Purpose and status of this letter

This process letter sets out the procedures for the next phase of Project Atlas. It is not an offer or invitation to enter
into any contract and does not form the basis of any contract. The Company and the Adviser reserve the right to
amend the process, extend or shorten any deadline, or terminate discussions with any Bidder at any time.

4. Indicative offer requirements

• Headline enterprise value and the assumptions underpinning it, including treatment of net debt, working
capital and any required reserves or indemnities.
• Form of consideration, financing sources and conditionality.
• Key assumptions on the user base, advertising revenue and synergy expectations.
• Confirmation of approvals required and an indicative timetable to signing.

6. Diligence rules

• All diligence is conducted through this virtual data room and the Q&A; module only.
• Bidders must not contact the Company’s employees, customers, suppliers or advisers directly. Breach may
result in exclusion from the process.

• Certain competitively sensitive and user-level-SE personal data are made available only to approved clean-team-SE
members under the protocol at DR-003-SE.
•
Information is provided for diligence only and remains subject to the NDA.

9. Clean-team-SE arrangements

Given the personal-data-SE and competitively sensitive nature of certain materials (audience data, advertiser pricing
and user-level-SE metrics), access to those materials is restricted to nominated clean-team-SE members who have
countersigned the clean-team-SE protocol. Clean-team-SE outputs may be shared with the wider deal team only in
aggregated or redacted form.
