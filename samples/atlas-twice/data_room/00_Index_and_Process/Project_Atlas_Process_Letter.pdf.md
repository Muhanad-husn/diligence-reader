Strictly private and confidential. Addressed to approved bidders in connection with the proposed acquisition of the

by Canton Street Capital as financial adviser to the Company (the “Adviser”).

1. Purpose and status of this letter

This process letter sets out the procedures for the next phase of Project Atlas. It is not an offer or invitation to enter
into any contract and does not form the basis of any contract. The Company and the Adviser reserve the right to
amend the process, extend or shorten any deadline, or terminate discussions with any Bidder at any time.

3. Indicative offer requirements

• Headline enterprise value and the assumptions underpinning it, including treatment of net debt, working
capital and any required reserves or indemnities.
• Form of consideration, financing sources and conditionality.
• Key assumptions on the user base, advertising revenue and synergy expectations.
• Confirmation of approvals required and an indicative timetable to signing.

4. Diligence rules

• All diligence is conducted through this virtual data room and the Q&A; module only.
• Bidders must not contact the Company’s employees, customers, suppliers or advisers directly. Breach may
result in exclusion from the process.

• Certain competitively sensitive and user-level personal data are made available only to approved clean-team
members under the protocol at DR-003.
•
Information is provided for diligence only and remains subject to the NDA.

Questions should be submitted through the Q&A; module and will be allocated to the relevant workstream.
Responses are shared at the Company’s discretion. A running log of questions and answers is maintained at
DR-004. Bidders should not rely on oral statements; only written data room materials and Q&A; responses may be
relied upon, subject to the terms of the definitive agreement.

6. Clean-team arrangements

Given the personal-data and competitively sensitive nature of certain materials (audience data, advertiser pricing
and user-level metrics), access to those materials is restricted to nominated clean-team members who have
countersigned the clean-team protocol. Clean-team outputs may be shared with the wider deal team only in
aggregated or redacted form.
