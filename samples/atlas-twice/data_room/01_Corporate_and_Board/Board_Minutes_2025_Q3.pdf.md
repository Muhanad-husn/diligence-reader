Meeting Meeting of the Board of Directors of VistaPort Media Inc.
Date & time 14 August 2025, 14:00 (held by video-conference)
Chair Margaret Ellison
Secretary Diane Foster, Corporate Secretary
Quorum A quorum being present, the Chair declared the meeting open.

Apologies: Catherine Voss sent apologies, which were noted.

Minutes of the previous meeting. The minutes of the Q2 2025 meeting, having been circulated, were approved
as a correct record and signed by the Chair. There were no matters arising not otherwise covered by the agenda.

1. Trading update

The Chief Financial Officer reported trading broadly in line with plan through the third quarter. The Board noted
continued strength in subscriptions and a stable advertising market, and confirmed the full-year revenue
expectation of approximately $5.15bn remained appropriate.

2. Strategic alternatives and inbound interest

The VP Corporate Development reported growing inbound interest from potential strategic acquirers, attracted by
the Company’s scale, mobile reach and advertising-technology assets. The Board discussed the user-base
monetisation thesis and the strategic value of a combination with a complementary telecoms customer base, and
supported continued preparation of the data room.

3. Technology modernisation

The Board received an update on the technology-modernisation programme. Decommissioning of legacy platforms
was reported as broadly on track. Management noted, in passing, that the decommission of the legacy unified
account profile store (legacy_uap) had been re-phased and was now expected to complete in 2026 to manage
migration dependencies into the AtlasID platform. The Board noted the update.

4. Risk and compliance

A routine risk and compliance update was received. The privacy programme and information-security assurance
were reported as operating effectively. No material matters were raised at this meeting.

5. People

The Board reviewed progress on the key-employee retention framework and noted no significant senior-leadership
changes in the quarter.

Any other business and close
