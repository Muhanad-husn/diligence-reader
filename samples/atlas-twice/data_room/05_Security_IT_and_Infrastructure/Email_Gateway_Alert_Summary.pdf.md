2. Notable campaigns

• A credential-harvesting phishing campaign impersonating a payroll provider was blocked; all 4,200 messages
quarantined; no user reported credential entry.

• A BEC wave impersonating the CFO was detected and quarantined; finance staff briefed.
• A malware campaign using password-protected archives was intercepted by sandbox detonation.
