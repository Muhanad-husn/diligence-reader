Monthly Finance Pack — November 2025. Management accounts and key performance indicators for VistaPort
Media Inc.. Prepared by Victor Osei, Group Controller. Figures are management estimates, unaudited.

1. Executive summary

November was affected by short-term operational friction associated with the Trust Reset programme (staged
password resets and session refresh). This temporarily reduced advertising impressions and email engagement
and drove a spike in customer-support contacts relating to account lockouts and password resets. Third-party
professional fees remained elevated. Management regards these effects as temporary and operational, and
expects engagement to recover.

$11m for the month, comprising lower delivered advertising impressions and email engagement together with
incremental customer-support cost. The adjustment is presented as a temporary, programme-related item and is
excluded from the underlying run-rate management monitors internally.
