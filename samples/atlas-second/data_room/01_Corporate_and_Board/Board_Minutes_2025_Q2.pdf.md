Meeting Meeting of the Board of Directors of VistaPort Media Inc.
Date & time 15 May 2025, 14:00 (held by video-conference)
Chair Margaret Ellison
Secretary Diane Foster, Corporate Secretary
Quorum A quorum being present, the Chair declared the meeting open.

Apologies: Yuki Tanaka sent apologies, which were noted.

Minutes of the previous meeting. The minutes of the Q1 2025 meeting, having been circulated, were approved
as a correct record and signed by the Chair. There were no matters arising not otherwise covered by the agenda.

1. Trading update

The Chief Financial Officer reported H1 FY2025 trading in line with plan, with revenue tracking towards the full-year
expectation. Advertising yield was stable and the subscriptions base continued to expand.

2. Strategic process — adviser appointment

Following the preliminary discussion at the previous meeting and a competitive selection process, the Board
approved the appointment of Canton Street Capital as financial adviser to assist the Company in evaluating
strategic alternatives, including a potential sale of the operating business. The Board noted the engagement letter
had been reviewed by the General Counsel.

1 RESOLVED, that Canton Street Capital be engaged as financial adviser on the terms reviewed by the
General Counsel.

2 RESOLVED, that management be authorised to commence preparation of diligence materials and a virtual
data room, subject to appropriate confidentiality controls.

3. Data-room readiness

The VP Corporate Development outlined the workplan for data-room readiness, including financial, commercial,
technology, legal and HR workstreams, and a clean-team protocol for user-level and competitively sensitive data.
The Board emphasised the importance of accuracy and completeness of diligence materials.

4. Risk and people

A routine risk update was received; no new material risks were reported. The Board noted a retention framework
would be developed for key employees ahead of any process.

Any other business and close

There being no other business, the Chair thanked attendees and declared the meeting closed. The next scheduled
meeting of the Board is to be held on 14 August 2025.
