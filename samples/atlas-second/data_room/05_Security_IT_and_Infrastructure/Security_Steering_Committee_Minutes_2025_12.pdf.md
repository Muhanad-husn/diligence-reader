1. Standing security operations

The Committee noted routine operational metrics: SOC alert volumes within normal range, patch SLAs on track,
and the email-gateway summary unremarkable. The penetration-test and SOC 2 actions are progressing. No
further discussion required.

2. AURORA workstream — status

IronLake provided a status update on the AURORA workstream. The Phase 1 technical draft findings and the final
executive summary were both before the Committee. The Committee noted the difference in emphasis between
the draft and the final summary and the importance of reading the final summary alongside the underlying
privileged work product. Counsel reminded members to preserve privilege and to avoid speculative
characterisation in non-privileged channels.

3. Notification and regulator engagement — discussion
A substantive discussion followed on whether, and how quickly, to broaden user notification and to engage
regulators. Positions were recorded as follows:

4. Insurance notice

The Committee considered the status of notice under the cyber programme via the broker (Redbridge). It was
noted that the position on the notice window required prompt resolution and that the wording of any notice
(including how the matter is characterised) was being coordinated with counsel. The Committee asked Finance
and Legal to confirm the position.

5. Dissent recorded

The Chair, Owen Bell, asked that his dissent be recorded. He stated that, in his professional view, the technical
evidence supported earlier and broader notification than the consensus position, and that he was uncomfortable
with the degree of caution being applied to external steps. The Committee acknowledged the dissent.
