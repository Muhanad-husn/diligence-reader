# Diligence Findings Report: Northwind Logistics Software

## 1. Executive Summary

**Recommendation: Proceed with conditions, subject to Q&A clarification.**

Northwind Logistics Software presents an attractive SaaS platform with strong recurring revenue and a diversified customer base. Diligence has identified several discrete issues across customer concentration, change-of-control exposure, revenue quality, and operational workstreams. The most significant is Granite Manufacturing's consent-based change-of-control provision in its customer MSA, which should be addressed as a pre-close condition. Other identified items are manageable through standard due diligence follow-up and deal documentation.

Summit should proceed with the transaction, subject to satisfactory Q&A responses on change-of-control consent, revenue recognition on the Cobalt prepayment, customer concentration dynamics, and EU data-processing posture.

---

## 2. Key Findings, Ranked by Materiality

### A. Granite Manufacturing change-of-control consent requirement

Granite Manufacturing Co. (sample_data_room/Northwind_Logistics/msa_granite_manufacturing.pdf.md, Section 10.2) holds a change-of-control provision requiring the non-assigning party provide prior written consent before assignment in connection with a change of control. Consent shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed.

This is a standard protective provision but represents a pre-close item requiring active engagement with Granite to secure consent prior to close.

### B. Meridian Freight customer concentration

Meridian Freight Corporation is the largest customer, at just under a third of recurring revenue (sample_data_room/Northwind_Logistics/arr_schedule.xlsx.md). The relationship is material and warrants close attention during integration. The Meridian MSA is a multi-year contract through 2027 billed annually in advance, representing a high-switching-cost relationship.

### C. Cobalt Retail multi-year prepaid order

Cobalt Retail Group represents a significant account under a three-year prepaid order totaling $28.8M in contract value (sample_data_room/Northwind_Logistics/order_form_cobalt_retail.pdf.md). The prepayment was collected in full in January 2025, creating strong cash inflow and revenue visibility.

The company should confirm that ASC 606 revenue recognition is correct for the three-year arrangement and that deferred revenue balances properly reflect the remaining service obligation.

### D. Harbor Foods termination for convenience

Harbor Foods Distribution represents an important mid-market customer with about a sixth of total ARR (sample_data_room/Northwind_Logistics/arr_schedule.xlsx.md). The Harbor MSA (sample_data_room/Northwind_Logistics/msa_harbor_foods.pdf.md) includes a termination-for-convenience clause requiring 30 days' notice. This is routine commercial risk applicable to most SaaS customers.

### E. Tidewater Distribution EU data-processing

Tidewater Distribution N.V. is an EU customer (sample_data_room/Northwind_Logistics/dpa_tidewater.pdf.md). The company has executed a Data Processing Addendum with appropriate terms covering EU personal data, including standard GDPR-compliant sub-processor provisions.

Buyer should confirm that the sub-processor register (sample_data_room/Northwind_Logistics/subprocessor_register.pdf.md) is current and that all sub-processors are properly documented.

### F. Core platform IP: contractor-built route-optimization engine

The platform incorporates a route-optimization engine built by an independent contractor (sample_data_room/Northwind_Logistics/contractor_agreement_route_engine.pdf.md). The company has secured a license to the contractor's work product. Buyer should confirm the scope and terms of the license agreement to ensure that the license is perpetual and permits the modifications required for platform development.

The company's Employee Proprietary Information and Inventions Assignment Agreement (sample_data_room/Northwind_Logistics/employment_ip_agreement.pdf.md) provides that employees assign work product to the company, creating a contrast with contractor arrangements.

---

## 3. Most Material Issue: Change-of-Control Consent and Deal Action

The Granite Manufacturing MSA contains a change-of-control consent provision that represents the primary customer-level deal condition. Under Section 10.2, consent is required but shall not be unreasonably withheld. This is a standard protective clause and is manageable through direct engagement.

### Recommended Deal Action

**Pre-Close Conditions:**

1. Obtain written consent from Granite Manufacturing to the proposed change of control.

2. Confirm in Q&A that Granite's consent requirement is satisfied and that no other material change-of-control consent items exist in the customer contract portfolio.

3. For Meridian Freight, confirm via customer reference check that the relationship remains stable through the proposed close and integration.

Buyer should develop a customer-success integration plan for each of the top-five accounts and schedule post-close business reviews within 30 days of close.

---

## 4. Lesser and Lower-Priority Issues

### Revenue recognition on Cobalt prepayment

The company should confirm ASC 606 treatment on the Cobalt prepaid order. Confirmation that revenue is being recognized ratable over the service period, and that deferred revenue balances align with remaining obligations, is a financial-statement quality item.

### Tidewater sub-processor documentation

Buyer should confirm that the sub-processor register is current and that any new sub-processors identified during integration have been properly approved.

### Route-optimization engine licensing

The contractor-built route-optimization engine is licensed to the company. Confirmation that the license is perpetual and permits derivative modifications for platform evolution is prudent.

---

## 5. Confidence and Open Items

### High confidence

Granite Manufacturing's change-of-control consent requirement is documented and is a standard protective provision. The consent standard (not to be unreasonably withheld) is market-standard and should be readily obtainable.

### Would confirm in Q&A

1. **Granite consent.** Status and timing of Granite's consent to change of control.

2. **Meridian stability.** Confirm via Meridian reference call that the customer intends to continue the relationship post-close.

3. **Cobalt revenue recognition.** Detailed ASC 606 analysis of revenue recognition on the prepaid order.

4. **Sub-processor register.** Current status of the sub-processor register.

5. **Contractor IP scope.** Confirmation of the scope and duration of the route-optimization engine license.

### Conclusion

The data room supports a proceed recommendation at the proposed valuation, subject to satisfactory Q&A responses on the items noted above. Granite Manufacturing's consent requirement is the primary pre-close condition. All other identified items are ordinary diligence findings and do not materially impair the deal thesis.

Proceed with the transaction.
