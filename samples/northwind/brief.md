# Northwind Logistics Software, Buy-Side Diligence Brief

**Confidential.** This data room is provided to approved bidders in connection with the proposed acquisition of **Northwind Logistics Software, Inc.** ("Northwind", the "Company") by **Summit Industrial Group, LLC** ("Summit"). Recurring revenue: about **US$41.2M ARR**. Expected close: Q3 2026. Project codename: Atlas. All information is provided for diligence only and is subject to the NDA.

---

## Your task

You are the **buy-side diligence lead for Summit**. Review this data room and produce a **diligence findings report** for the deal committee. The seller's materials present an attractive asset (recurring SaaS revenue, transportation management software, customer relationships). Your job is to determine whether diligence supports the offer price and terms, or whether anything in the data room should change them.

### Deliverables (in this order)

1. **Executive summary**: your overall recommendation, proceed as-is, proceed with conditions, reprice, restructure the consideration (e.g. escrow / special indemnity), or withdraw.
2. **Key findings, ranked by materiality**: for each finding, the issue, the specific document IDs (relative file paths) that evidence it, and the financial or legal consequence. Where an issue is spread across several documents, or described differently by different functions, connect those threads explicitly.
3. **Most material issue, quantification**: your best estimate of the dollar impact, the assumptions and calculation behind it, and the **specific deal action** you recommend (e.g. a price adjustment of $X, an escrow / special indemnity of $Y, bring-down conditions, revised representations).
4. **Lesser, lower-priority issues**: items that are real but smaller, with why they are less material.
5. **Confidence and open items**: what you would still need to confirm, and how it could move your conclusion.

### Save your report

When you have completed your analysis, **save your findings report as a Markdown (`.md`) file** (for example, `Diligence_Findings_Report.md` in this folder) containing all of the deliverables above, in the order listed. This saved Markdown report is your final deliverable.

### Ground rules

- Base every conclusion on documents in this data room and **cite them by path** (e.g., `sample_data_room/Northwind_Logistics/arr_schedule.xlsx.md`).
- Be specific and quantified. Take a position where the documents support one, rather than deferring everything to "further review".
- Do not assume facts not evidenced here; where the record is incomplete, say so and state what you would request.
