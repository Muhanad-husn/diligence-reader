# Acquisition checklist

The fixed checklist every room is ranked against. Each item is one yes or no question about a single contract; yes means the contract bears on the item. It is the contract-answerable part of two published buy-side legal due diligence checklists: Harvey, M&A due diligence checklist, https://www.harvey.ai/blog/m-and-a-due-diligence-checklist, and Vaquill, M&A due diligence legal workstream checklist, https://www.vaquill.ai/blog/ma-due-diligence-legal-workstream-checklist. Items that need financial statements, tax returns or site visits are left out. Written 2026-10-05, before any test, and the same for every room.

Deal types: share, asset. A share deal covers a merger or a purchase of the company's shares. An asset deal covers a purchase of the business's assets.

## Material contracts

- change-of-control [share, asset]: Does this contract give a party a right to terminate, consent or be paid on a change of control of the company?
- assignment-restriction [share, asset]: Does this contract restrict its assignment or require consent to transfer it?
- mfn-pricing [share, asset]: Does this contract give a party most favoured customer or most favoured nation terms?
- exclusivity [share, asset]: Does this contract grant exclusive rights or oblige a party to deal exclusively?
- non-compete [share, asset]: Does this contract restrict the company from competing in a market, territory or line of business?
- termination-convenience [share, asset]: Does this contract let a party terminate without cause?
- minimum-commitment [share, asset]: Does this contract commit the company to minimum purchases, volumes or payments?
- source-code-escrow [share, asset]: Does this contract put source code or other technology in escrow for a third party?

## Acquisitions and dispositions

- acquisition-agreement [share, asset]: Is this contract an agreement to buy, sell or merge a business, company or line of assets?
- contingent-payment [share, asset]: Does this contract provide for an earn-out, deferred or contingent purchase price?
- surviving-indemnity [share, asset]: Does this contract contain indemnification obligations of the company that survive closing or termination?

## Debt and security

- debt [share, asset]: Is this contract a loan, credit facility, note or other borrowing by the company?
- financial-covenants [share]: Does this contract impose financial covenants on the company?
- debt-change-of-control [share]: Does this contract require repayment of debt or count a change of control as a default?
- cross-default [share]: Does this contract treat a default under another agreement as a default under it?
- security-interest [share, asset]: Does this contract grant a lien or security interest over company assets?
- guarantee [share, asset]: Does this contract make the company a guarantor of another party's obligations?

## Equity and dilution

- equity-issuance [share]: Does this contract issue or agree to issue shares, warrants, options or convertible securities?
- anti-dilution [share]: Does this contract adjust a holder's price or number of shares on a later issuance?
- registration-rights [share]: Does this contract give a holder registration rights?
- investor-rights [share]: Does this contract give an investor board seats, vetoes, pre-emptive rights or rights of first refusal?

## Employment and benefits

- change-of-control-payment [share, asset]: Does this contract pay severance or a bonus to an employee or officer on a change of control?
- equity-acceleration [share]: Does this contract accelerate vesting of equity awards on a change of control?
- executive-agreement [share, asset]: Is this contract an employment, severance or retention agreement with an officer or director?
- employee-restrictive-covenant [share, asset]: Does this contract bind an employee or founder to a non-compete or non-solicit?

## Intellectual property

- ip-ownership [share, asset]: Does this contract assign or allocate ownership of intellectual property?
- ip-license-in [share, asset]: Does this contract license to the company intellectual property it depends on?
- ip-license-out [share, asset]: Does this contract license company intellectual property to a third party?

## Real estate

- lease [share, asset]: Is this contract a lease of real property by the company?

## Litigation and disputes

- settlement [share, asset]: Does this contract settle or release a claim, dispute or litigation?
- uncapped-liability [share, asset]: Does this contract leave the company's liability uncapped?

## Regulatory

- government-contract [share, asset]: Is the company's counterparty in this contract a government or government agency?
- regulatory-approval [share, asset]: Does this contract depend on a regulatory licence, permit, clearance or approval?

## Related parties

- related-party [share, asset]: Is the counterparty in this contract an affiliate, officer, director or major shareholder of the company?
